# Product

## Platform

web

## Stack

Delegated: a single static HTML file (with inline CSS and minimal JS), hosted on Firebase Hosting. A one-page portfolio needs no framework.

## Users

- Recruiters and hiring managers deciding whether to interview Vaibhav for senior full-stack roles. They are skimming, often on a phone, and need proof fast.
- Founders and investors judging him as a builder or co-founder. They care about the founder story: what he built, what broke, and what he learned.

## Product Purpose

A single-page personal portfolio for Vaibhav Pal: a senior full-stack engineer with over 4 years of experience who has also founded two startups. Success means a visitor understands both sides of him (shipping engineer and founder) and reaches out.

## Positioning

He is an engineer who has run real businesses, including one that generated revenue, and who is open about the failures. The failures are presented as evidence of judgment, not hidden.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- One page, no CMS. LinkedIn posts are out of scope.
- Hosted on Firebase.

## Product Principles

1. Honesty over polish: failures are told plainly, with the lesson.
2. Proof over adjectives: lead with real numbers like ₹3L MRR, the $15M valuation, and sub-3-second load times.
3. Scannable in 30 seconds for a recruiter, with more depth for anyone who wants it.
